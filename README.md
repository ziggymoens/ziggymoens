I design and deliver **Odoo/ERP implementations** for companies that have outgrown their spreadsheets.
My background is full-stack development, which means I sit comfortably on both sides of the table: the
business conversation about *what the process should be*, and the technical one about *how it actually gets built*.

- 🏗️ Currently **Solution Architect Odoo** at [dear digital](https://www.deardigital.com) and owner of **Zigmo Studio**
- 🎓 Just finished an **MSc in IT Project Management** at Stockholm University, with a thesis on stakeholder involvement versus real influence during ERP transformations
- 🖥️ Previously led the **Odoo/ERP business unit** at Endare, after starting there as a full-stack developer
- 🏊‍♂️🚴‍♂️🏃‍♂️ IRONMAN finisher, which is roughly the same skill set that makes long ERP projects survivable
